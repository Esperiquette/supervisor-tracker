How to use this tab
  • Oxford asks for three referees, and at least one must be academic. Aim for two or three academic letters, and add a professional one only if you need it.
  • Ask people who supervised your research or saw your best course work. Recent beats old. Asking for a "strong" letter lets them decline politely if they cannot write one.
  • The scholarship deadline is 2 December, so ask this week or next. After a yes, send a packet: CV, draft research proposal, project summaries, portfolio link and the deadline.
  • Suggested order: Bahr, Khoshnoud, Lakeh, then Bhandari. Caffrey and Mahto are backups, and Broadhead is the professional option.
  • Read the item each email mentions (an announcement or research page) before you send, so your question is a real one.
  • Follow up once after about a week, and thank each referee when their letter is in. The idea cards below are people worth considering, with no draft yet.
