Admissions facts for entry 2027-28 (checked 26 Oct 2026)
  • Engineering Science, MSc by Research: about 5 places and about 110 applications per year (three-year average). That is roughly 1 in 20. Scholarship deadline 2 December 2026 (12:00 UK time). Final deadline 2 March 2027. Supervisor contact is recommended; you can list up to four.
  • Biology, MSc by Research: about 49 places shared with the DPhil in Biology, about 34 applications per year (two-year average). Scholarship deadline 6 January 2027. Expected final deadline 2 March 2027 (may close earlier or reopen). The page says you must contact a potential supervisor before applying.
  • Fees 2027-28: Overseas 36,260 pounds a year (both courses); Home 10,940. Oxford expects over 1,200 full or partial scholarships across all graduate courses; I could not find how many go to MSc by Research students.
  • Entry: The Biology page asks US applicants for a GPA of at least 3.5. Your GPA is 3.47. I could not retrieve the Engineering requirement. A supervisor who backs you matters more because of this.
  • Department-wide, 2024-25 entry: Engineering Science research programmes 1,131 applications, 303 offers, 160 acceptances. Biology research 215, 77, 31. These include DPhils and I could not confirm which programmes they cover.
  • Only Papachristodoulou and Zavatsky (and Zhong You, from my earlier notes) say in writing that they take master's-level research students. Everyone else needs a direct question.
  • Sources: Engineering course page, Biology course page, 2024-25 admissions statistics.

How to use this tab
  • Oxford asks for three referees, and at least one must be academic. Aim for two or three academic letters, and add a professional one only if you need it.
  • Ask people who supervised your research or saw your best course work. Recent beats old. Asking for a "strong" letter lets them decline politely if they cannot write one.
  • The scholarship deadline is 2 December, so ask this week or next. After a yes, send a packet: CV, draft research proposal, project summaries, portfolio link and the deadline.
  • Suggested order: Bahr, Khoshnoud, Lakeh, then Bhandari. Caffrey and Mahto are backups, and Broadhead is the professional option.
  • Read the item each email mentions (an announcement or research page) before you send, so your question is a real one.
  • Follow up once after about a week, and thank each referee when their letter is in. The idea cards below are people worth considering, with no draft yet.
